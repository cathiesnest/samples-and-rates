How It Works

A straightforward process from your first message to final delivery.

1

Tell Us What You Need

Send your idea, requirements, references, or examples of what you're looking for.

2

Confirm the Scope

We'll clarify the project, what's included, expected timeline, and final price.

3

Initial Payment

Work begins once the agreed initial payment has been received.

4

Build & Review

We develop the agreed project and provide the revisions included in the scope.

5

Final Payment

Once the project is completed and approved, the remaining balance is settled.

6

Delivery

You receive the completed project and agreed files, access, or deployment details.

Payment & Terms

Clear expectations help keep every project simple and transparent.

  • Projects starting at ₱2,500 generally use a 50% deposit and 50% final payment arrangement.
  • Smaller projects may have different payment arrangements depending on the scope.
  • The quoted price covers the agreed project scope only.
  • Additional features or major changes may require an additional fee.
  • Client-provided content should be supplied within the agreed timeline.
  • Revisions are limited to those included in the agreed project scope.
  • Project timelines depend on complexity and timely client feedback.
  • Final files, access, or deployment are provided after project completion and settlement of the remaining balance.
